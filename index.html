TASPO Taskbook

The Digital Taskbook is on its way.

Inakuja hivi karibuni.

A simple app where students plan their days, set goals, track their tasks and run their own projects, with teachers following their progress. We are building it now, and it will open first in a small group of pilot schools.

Our plan so far

  1. Plan what the Taskbook will do (done)
  2. Set up app.taspo.org (done)
  3. Design the screens with students and teachers In progress
  4. Build and test the app
  5. Open to the first pilot schools
